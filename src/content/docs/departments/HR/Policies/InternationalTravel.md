## 1. Purpose

Summit Technology Holdings, LLC (STH) recognizes that international travel introduces elevated risk to corporate technology assets and the sensitive data they contain. This policy establishes enterprise-wide requirements for employees who carry or use corporate devices while traveling internationally, ensuring those assets are appropriately secured before departure, during travel, and upon return.

---

## 2. Applicability

This policy applies to all employees and contractors of Summit Technology Holdings and its subsidiaries, including FrontRunnerHC, Inc. and LabXChange360, LLC, who travel internationally while in possession of corporate-issued technology assets, including laptops, mobile phones, and tablets. It applies regardless of whether the travel is for business or personal purposes.

---

## 3. Policy

### 3.1 Approval Process and Conditions

Company systems are configured to automatically block access from outside the United States. Employees who want to continue to work and support the organization using their corporate technology assets while on international travel must obtain approval from their manager, HR, and the IT department at least 15 days before their departure date.  As part of the pre-travel IT approval process, IT will configure a temporary exception for your trip so that you can continue to access company resources while abroad.

Employees must work with the IT department in advance of their planned travel to ensure their corporate technology assets are properly configured and prepared:

- Employees must understand how and when to use a VPN on their device for connecting to company resources.  This allows for secure connectivity to corporate resources especially when using un-trusted public Wi-Fi.
- The IT Department must ensure that the company device(s), including laptops and phones, are properly registered within the Mobile Device Management system.  This allows for a device to be remotely wiped in the event of loss or theft of the device.
- Employees must complete a short training video on the risks of working with corporate technology assets when abroad.

Employees must agree to the following conditions before taking their corporate technology assets for international travel:

- Employees must limit their use of corporate technology assets to essential work-related activities, such as checking emails, accessing files, or attending meetings.  Employees must not use their corporate technology assets for personal entertainment, social media, gaming, or other non-work-related purposes.
- Employees must not connect their corporate technology assets to any unsecured or public Wi-Fi networks without first activating the company VPN, or use any unauthorized or unencrypted communication channels, such as personal email accounts, messaging apps, or cloud services.
- Employees must not store any sensitive or confidential data on their corporate technology assets or transfer any data to or from any external devices, such as USB drives, SD cards, or personal phones.
- Employees must not access any websites, applications, or content that are illegal, inappropriate, or offensive in the destination countries, or that could damage the company’s reputation or violate its policies.
- Employees must report any loss, theft, damage, or compromise of their corporate technology assets to their manager and the IT department as soon as possible and follow the instructions for device recovery or remote wipe.
- Employees must return their corporate technology assets to the IT department for inspection and verification upon their return from their international travel.

---

## 4. Compliance & Governance

STH maintains records of international travel approvals, pre-travel device preparation activities, and post-travel inspections. The IT department and HR are jointly responsible for administering this policy. Non-compliance may result in remediation actions, revocation of
travel privileges for corporate devices, or escalation to executive management.  

---

## 5. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, contract suspension, or legal action depending on severity. Summit Technology Holdings reserves the right to revoke access privileges at any time to protect its systems and data.
